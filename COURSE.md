# Course guide

**Practical AI Engineering and Business Automation | October 2026 | 16 weeks**

This course turns one real or clearly fictional business problem into a checked, recoverable AI-assisted process. The [outline](course-outline.md) defines the design; the [chapters](course/README.md), exercises, fixtures, and assessments provide the teaching material.

## Audience and routes

The primary route is for engineers who can read and modify a small program. Basic Git, JSON, command-line, and testing knowledge is useful. Complete [preparation exercises](course/preparation.md) for gaps.

Business owners need no programming prerequisite. You will map a process, prepare evidence, configure a workflow, test acceptance criteria, select an agent platform, and establish operating responsibility. Use an implementation partner where installation or infrastructure exceeds your experience.

Both routes produce functioning work. A strategy presentation alone is insufficient. AI assistance is allowed, but learners must explain, verify, and revise their submission.

## Outcomes

By completion, learners can choose suitable AI tasks; write useful instructions and source contracts; check numerical and factual claims; review AI-assisted code; distinguish valid structure from correct meaning; build a reviewed workflow; explain retrieval and agent access; compare OpenClaw, Hermes, and Grok Bot; evaluate failures; calculate value including review effort; and hand over a bounded system.

Each chapter has three sections: concepts, a worked example or procedure, and a studio with two routes. These become three site lessons, giving 48 lessons and 128 open-book questions. Site reading estimates exclude independent lab time.

## Weekly pattern

Engineers should plan 8-10 hours: 1-2 reading, 4-5 implementing, 1-2 evaluating, and one reviewing. Owners should plan 5-7 hours: 1-2 reading, 2-3 configuring/testing, and two recording evidence and reviewing. These are planning estimates.

Use the same chosen process throughout. Start with Harbour Services when real data is unavailable. Keep synthetic timings and authored predictions clearly labelled; they do not establish workplace impact or model quality.

Implement one agent platform deeply. For the other two, supervised demonstrations or authentic instructor recordings can substitute for account access. If no run exists, mark the capability untested and use documentary comparison. Do not fabricate evidence.

## Completion

Practical grading is 30% weekly portfolio, 15% platform comparison, 45% capstone, and 10% demonstration/handover. Completion requires 70% overall and all four progression gates. See [assessments](ASSESSMENTS.md) and [capstone requirements](CAPSTONE.md).

The site separately grades eight knowledge questions per week, with a 70% threshold and three attempts. Quizzes and self-attestation can produce a site completion record; they do not establish independent practical assessment.

Advanced ML, model internals, fine-tuning, local inference, advanced retrieval, multiple agents, and voice are [optional extensions](course/extensions.md), selected to solve a measured problem after the core.
