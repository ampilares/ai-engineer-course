# Reusable task brief

Use these fields for each new task. The completed example is for the fictional Harbour case.

- **Outcome:** a checked weekly operations draft for Maya.
- **Inputs:** sales.csv, authorised tickets, approved policy passages, and the meeting transcript; reporting period 1-7 October 2026.
- **Procedure:** validate and reconcile inputs; calculate totals; retrieve effective policy; prepare a source-backed draft; expose unresolved items.
- **Output:** scope, checked totals, service issues, policy questions, and proposed actions.
- **Evidence:** transaction IDs for figures, ticket IDs for issues, policy IDs for rules, transcript timestamps for meeting actions.
- **Unknowns:** missing amounts, mixed requests, and unassigned owners remain unresolved.
- **Allowed actions:** read supplied inputs and prepare the draft.
- **Review:** Maya verifies the exact output before operational use.
- **Finish condition:** all numbers reconcile, sources support claims, and unresolved items have a visible next step.
- **Budget and stop:** stop on missing mandatory input, permission denial, or the agreed execution limit; report what is incomplete.

For your own project, replace each value with a specific decision. Avoid adjectives without a test: replace "high quality" with the exact facts or behaviours the reviewer can check.
