### Engineering route

1. Reproduce the final candidate from its documented setup and version record.
2. Run the agreed acceptance and boundary checks. Preserve the output and any unresolved limitations.
3. Demonstrate the four behaviours: success, failure, blocked action, and recovery.
4. Hand over the repository, configuration instructions, runbook, and maintenance ownership.
5. Explain the next improvement you would test and the evidence that would justify it.

### Business-owner route

1. Verify the workflow, staff procedure, review responsibilities, and manual fallback are usable by another person.
2. Present the measured or clearly simulated results and volume-sensitive business case.
3. Demonstrate how staff identify uncertainty, reject an action, and stop the workflow.
4. Name the operational owner, budget owner, and reviewer for future changes.
5. Choose adopt, revise, or stop and communicate the scope of that decision.
