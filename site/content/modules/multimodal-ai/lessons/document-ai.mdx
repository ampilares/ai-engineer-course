Documents are not bags of text. Meaning lives in headings, columns, labels, checkboxes, handwriting, stamps, page order, and the relationship between a table cell and its header. A document pipeline must preserve that structure before asking a model to reason over it.

## Classify, parse, then reason

A practical path is: validate file, split pages, classify document/page type, detect whether text is native or scanned, extract text and layout, identify tables/forms, normalize fields, validate, and route uncertainty. Use embedded PDF text when trustworthy; OCR scanned regions; apply specialist table or form extraction when structure matters.

Reading order is a first-class output. Two-column text flattened row-by-row becomes nonsense. Store blocks with page number, bounding box, type, reading-order index, and extraction confidence. Keep table cells with row/column spans and header relationships rather than converting them immediately to prose.

## Extract with evidence and constraints

Define a schema that includes value, normalized value, evidence page/region, and status such as `present`, `not_found`, or `illegible`. Apply deterministic constraints: totals reconcile, dates are plausible, identifiers match checksums, and currency is consistent. Cross-page documents may require grouping pages before extraction; classification should not treat page two as a separate unknown document.

Confidence can combine OCR certainty, evidence presence, validation results, document type, and measured model performance on similar layouts. A number that fails reconciliation should be reviewed even if the model reports high confidence.

## Documents change and can be hostile

Templates drift as vendors update software. Monitor quality by vendor/layout, scan quality, language, and page count. Create a “new layout” route instead of forcing every unfamiliar page through the dominant template. Treat document text as untrusted: a résumé, invoice note, or white-on-white PDF text can contain prompt injection. Embedded scripts, links, attachments, and metadata expand the attack surface.

## Guided build: a document evidence pipeline

Select at least 30 documents across three layouts, including scans, tables, rotated pages, missing fields, handwriting or low-quality images, and one injected instruction. Build classification, text/layout extraction, typed field extraction, evidence linking, validation, and review routing.

Measure character/word accuracy on a sample, document classification, field-level exact or normalized match, table-cell correctness, evidence-region validity, reconciliation pass, review rate, latency, and cost. Report results by layout and scan quality. The artifact passes when a reviewer can click or locate the exact page region for every accepted critical field and unsafe instructions cannot trigger actions.

<Callout type="tip">
Keep a small visual regression set. Text-only assertions cannot reveal that a parser suddenly reversed columns, clipped a footer, or shifted every bounding box after a rendering-library update.
</Callout>
